About this data

Tiers, specs and notes come straight from the PSU tier list spreadsheet and are refreshed daily. Read the spreadsheet's own notes for what each tier means. Ratings marked * are limited confidence: the maintainers had too little information to rate the unit reliably, and they don't recommend these units until more is known.

Prices come from the public listings of Canadian stores (currently Best Buy and Canada Computers) and are refreshed daily. Listings are matched to the tier list by name, so a match can occasionally be the wrong revision of a PSU. Always check that the listing's model matches before you buy. Each PSU also links to searches at other Canadian stores.
